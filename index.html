About

The short version

Summary

Most reporting problems aren't analysis problems. They're a column of dates stored four different ways.

I started where a lot of analysts do — inside other people's spreadsheets. As an MIS Executive I owned the daily, weekly and monthly reporting pack: pulling the raw exports, cleaning them, validating the numbers, and rebuilding the whole thing as templates so the next month took hours instead of days.

Before that, my internship at NexGen Analytix was the analytical half of the same job — exploratory analysis in Python, SQL against relational databases, and Power BI dashboards that gave the business side something to point at in a meeting.

What I'm after now is a team where the reporting is load-bearing: where someone actually decides something because of the dashboard. I'm comfortable being the person who asks what the number is for before building it.
